A preview

The six repairs that cover most failures.

You don't need a trade certificate. You need to recognize these, in order, every time.

$5–15

Lid / door switch

Check this first if the machine won't start at all.

$10–25

Drive belt

Worn or snapped belts cause "won't spin" symptoms.

$15–35

Water inlet valve

The usual cause when a machine won't fill.

$20–45

Drain pump

Fixes "won't drain" and standing-water issues.

$10–20

Coupler / clutch

A common wear part on direct-drive top-loaders.

$0–10

Cord, fuse, cutoff

The cheapest possible fix — always check it first.

Before you buy

A few honest questions.

Do I need any repair experience?
No. The guide is written for someone who has never opened an appliance before, and points you to free model-specific videos for anything not covered directly.
How much money do I need to start?
Most people start with $150–$300: enough for one or two machines, parts, and a basic tool kit. The guide shows how to reinvest from there.
Does this work outside the US?
The core method — sourcing, repairing, renting — applies broadly, but pricing examples and marketplace names reference the US. Adjust sourcing channels to your local market.
What format is the guide?
A clean, printable digital document delivered immediately after purchase.
